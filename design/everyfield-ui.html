Design systemFoundations

Foundations

Brand, color roles, type, shape and patterns. Everything else is built from these tokens. Dark theme uses the same token names with dark values (switch with the sun and moon in the rail).

Tokens v0.1

Logo

#ef-mark · #ef-wordmark
In the app: ink mark, no background (top bar). Chips below are for avatars, favicons and social images.

Brand colors

--ef-ink · --ef-green · --ef-cream

The app runs on neutral greys so the data hues stand out. Ink carries the brand inside the app. Field green is for the logo and rare brand moments. Cream is for the landing page, marketing and print.

Ink#181D19 · text, active nav, primary button
Field green#1CE362 · logo, brand moments and the green 500 step
Cream#F6F0E9 · brand and marketing only, glow in gradients

Data hues and roles

--ef-{hue}-{600|500|300|100|50}

Every hue has one job, and each ramp is built from a gradient tone. Green (Field, the brand green) means healthy or committed. Blue (Sky) means measured or actual. Rose means risk or a gap. Orange (Dawn) means attention or waiting. Lilac and teal only label categories, like pipeline stages. Steps 500 to 50 come from the gradient colors; 600 and 700 are deeper shades for text that reach 4.5:1. Tints are light and clean, never grey. Token names stay generic (green, blue, magenta, amber) so a hue can change without renaming.

Content width

--ef-content-max · --ef-content-max-wide · data-width="wide"

The top bar and the rail always span the full window. The content column stops at 1536px and sits in the center of the space to the right of the rail. On narrower screens it fills that space, so 1440px screens look the same as before. Dense list pages (People, Meetings, Documents) set data-width="wide" on their page section and can grow to 1920px. The router copies that value to body[data-width] on each page change. Sign-in and onboarding have no cap.

Phase 1 read3 of 6

A vision meeting is a short, warm gathering where you share why this church should exist. The goal is not a crowd. The goal is people who leave asking how they can help.

Before the meeting

Pick a home with room for about 40 adults. Ask two or three core members to host with you, and give each host a short list of people to invite in person.

  • Print response cards and a guest sign-in sheet.
  • Plan childcare so parents can listen.
  • Keep the agenda to 90 minutes, food included.
Playbook rhythm

Hold a vision meeting at least every 14 days. A steady rhythm matters more than a big one.

During the meeting

Tell the story of the place and the people you hope to reach. Be honest about what you do not know yet. Leave time for questions.

"Where there is no vision, the people perish."Proverbs 29:18

Response cards

Close by handing out response cards. People can mark that they want to learn more, want to pray, or want to join the core group.

After the meeting

Call every first-time guest within 48 hours. Everyfield creates these follow-ups for you when you finalize attendance.

Questions people ask

What is the difference between the Wiki and Help?

The Wiki teaches the church-planting method: what to do in each phase and why. Help explains how to use Everyfield: where to click and what each screen does.

Can Evry change my records on its own?

No. Evry can read and draft. Every change it suggests waits for you to review and approve it.

Who can see the people in my church?

Only people with a seat in your church. A sending church or network never sees names or personal details.

Can I undo a merge or a sent email?

No. Everyfield warns you before these actions. You can correct details later, and the history keeps what changed.

Why does a count show “Not recorded” instead of 0?

“Not recorded” means nobody entered a number yet. A 0 is a real result, for example a meeting with no guests.

Record attendance at the door

3 min·Works on a phone·Updated Oct 2026

Do it now

Check people in as they arrive, so you know who came for the first time and who needs a follow-up in the next 48 hours.

Steps

  1. Open the meeting.

    Go to Meetings and choose today's meeting.

  2. Start check-in.

    Press Start check-in. On a phone, the list fills the screen and the search sits at the top.

  3. Tap each person who arrives.

    A green tick shows they are present. Tap again to undo a wrong tap before you finish.

  4. Add a first-time guest.

    Press New guest, type a name and an email or phone. Everyfield checks for a possible duplicate.

  5. Finish.

    Press Finish check-in. First-time guests get a follow-up task, due in 48 hours.

Tip

If nobody new came, record 0 guests. An empty count means “not recorded”, which is different from zero.
