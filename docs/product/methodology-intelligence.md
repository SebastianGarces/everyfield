## Methodology and authority

Plant Intelligence interprets progress toward launch under the preserved planting methodology. It does not assess overall church health, character, doctrine, pastoral care or spiritual qualification. Preserve the original [Playbook](../reference/playbook.md), [reviewed rubric v1](../reference/rubric-v1.md), [historical v0](../reference/rubric-v0.md) and [SME comment dispositions](../reference/bryan-comments-review.md) byte for byte. The preserved executable prompt is source evidence; its stale open-C12 wording does not overturn the final biweekly ruling. Current evidence operationalization uses [plant-operational-adapter-v1.json](../reference/plant-operational-adapter-v1.json) and does not rewrite prior assessments.

The phases are 0 Discovery, 1 Core Group Development, 2 Launch Team Formation, 3 Training & Preparation, 4 Pre-Launch, 5 Launch Sunday and 6 Post-Launch. The eight lenses are Vision Casting; Shared Ownership; Critical Mass; Core Group Cohesion; Prayer; Generosity and Financial Readiness; Emerging Leadership; Comprehensive Training. The eight ministry responsibilities are Worship, Children's, Assimilation, Small Groups, Administration/Finance, Facilities, Promotion and Technology. Prayer Leader is a separate coverage responsibility. Operational team templates are optional selected imports; custom structure remains supported and omitted imports do not erase methodology responsibilities. An eligible person can cover several responsibilities; show the distinct-person count separately from filled slots.

The 4 C's are Committed, Compelled, Contagious and Courageous. Interview considerations are maturity, giftedness, chemistry, right reasons and season of life. These remain recorded human judgments. Models can cite an authorized dated judgment, never invent it or infer spiritual fitness from attendance, serving or role assignment. Operational reference assistance can locate existing content; Evry does not generate prayer, theological counsel or pastoral decisions.

Owner and accepted Admin share ordinary plant administration and current plant-intelligence reading. A CRM lead title adds no grant. Other established role projections stay individually scoped. Organization access uses the exact receiving organization and current affiliation/grants plus the applicable sharing policy; an organization role never makes its holder a plant editor. Every durable Evry request, feedback, attestation, phase or settings write uses the native shared operation and exact human review. Private care has no Evry adapter.

Preserve actual reader, canonical article, accessible published source/revision and progress. A different eligible reader's complete six-item set can establish completion. Two readers' disjoint halves cannot. A lead title supplies no body entitlement or account grant. Neither exercises nor whole-team reading is required.

An authorized initial declaration records the phase a pre-existing journey had reached; it does not synthesize earlier phases. One initial declaration per plant has a separate retry-safe recorded outcome. Both declaration and ordinary transition reject non-numeric, non-integer or out-of-range phase values: only integers 0–6 are valid. Ordinary forward, backward, skipped or corrective transitions require a string reason trimmed to 1–2,000 characters, current authority and unchanged reviewed plant state. After trimming, reject the reserved initial-declaration reason `Declared at setup — where this plant already was when it joined EveryField.` in every ordinary transition, including same-phase requests. Declaration accepts no caller reason and stores that exact sentence server-side with kind `initial_declaration`; ordinary requests cannot supply that kind. The stored kind, never reason text, distinguishes declaration from movement. Detect concurrent changes and derive the prior phase from current state. Preserve direction, actor, occurrence/recording time and journal. Same-phase requests retain the source's `noop` journal/event receipt, with no assessment input change, fake progression or automatic task creation; an exact replay returns that receipt without another entry. Phase changes offer the existing task templates and Wiki recommendations. A template offer never silently creates all tasks or completes work. Advisory readiness never blocks, advances or regresses phase.

## Deterministic facts and evidence

Every snapshot has one immutable run `asOf`, source revision, schema/formula version and typed provenance. Recorded occurrence, deterministic calculation, dated human attestation and model interpretation remain distinct. Unknown, a real zero, a recorded false answer, an unanswered question and a retracted answer are different states. A number, date, percentage or countdown in output must resolve to an exact persisted fact/citation. Current corrections refresh current projections while historical snapshots remain attributable.

Current adult size counts one live same-plant person who is an effective adult (from the birth date, or the recorded classification when there is no birth date) with authoritative active commitment, across core-group and launch-team evidence. Repeated signatures, several teams and roles count once. Children, withdrawn current commitments, deleted/merged-away identities and foreign records do not count. Household position, stage, title, login or ministry membership proves neither adulthood nor commitment. No DOB is required; a birth date, when present, decides adult or child. An otherwise eligible account-linked adult counts; an automatically created Owner contact alone supplies no commitment evidence.

Report the known adult lower bound and complete/partial/unavailable evidence separately. Thirty established eligible adults meet the ≥30 criterion even when other candidates remain unresolved; exact total stays null and partial evidence stays visible. With 29 known plus an unclassified candidate who could change the result, size is Unknown. A reliable maximum below 30 can establish Not met without claiming an exact total. Complete observed empty data can be exact zero; an unavailable read cannot.

Growth measures genuine historical first adult acquisitions independently of current size. Use each canonical person's first valid actual commitment occurrence, not recording/edit time. A real later withdrawal lowers current size but preserves that acquisition in the 28-day windows and last-acquisition clock. A correction invalidating an erroneous acquisition or correcting its date recomputes current growth through attributable history. Reactivation, repeat signatures, team changes and relinking do not create a new adult. Canonical merge deduplicates the human and retains the earliest valid acquisition. Classification correction uses the established historical occurrence; it does not invent an acquisition today. No established date means timing Unknown. Current evidence can prove a recent acquisition while exact total/clock remains partial; an old known date cannot prove stalled if unresolved evidence might contain a newer acquisition.

Use plant-local LocalDate at the snapshot asOf: the current acquisition window is [asOf−27, asOf], and the prior window is [asOf−55, asOf−28], each exactly 28 inclusive calendar dates. The flat clock uses calendar-date difference. Compare trailing 28 days with the preceding 28 days; this is acquisition count comparison, not current net size change. Below 21 days do not say slowed or stalled. At 21–27 days, “momentum has slowed” is permitted. “Growth has stalled” requires at least 28 days without a genuine new adult. The Phase 1 trajectory minimum holds below 28 days when evidence establishes it. The Playbook's 50–100 adult benchmarks and 30–40 next-phase cluster remain methodology benchmarks, never universal health verdicts. There is no invented forecast, growth cap or conversion claim. Growth-source composition stays a separate planter-only recorded-source reading with unknown-source count; organizations receive no composition of individual sources or cross-plant ranking.

Vision cadence uses actually completed Vision meetings, their actual occurrence and completed attendance: biweekly 14-day target, watch at 21, direct at 28; predictable weekly practice is legitimate. The last two attendance observations support their stated trend only. Cohesion uses the actual completed-meeting population: no attendance in the last 28 days after some in the prior 28; active committed denominator; at least 20% and at least three disengaged people. A recorded leader among the disengaged changes voice one level, not the person's qualification. No attendance evidence means Unknown; absence of Vision meetings alone cannot invalidate valid core-group-meeting evidence. Cohesion is an operational observation, not relational unity.

Follow-up ownership comes only from live task assignments; stale work cannot prove the planter carries it. A removed/ineligible assignee is unowned. Task due/overdue and Complete/No longer needed are distinct from actual successful contact. A date-only follow-up is due through originating meeting's local date plus two calendar days; the clearly labelled 48-hour originating-obligation TASK-completion measure uses its successful completion Instant within elapsed time from the actual originating occurrence instant. A valid recorded attempted-no-contact or administrative-completion outcome can fulfill the task and earn work-timeliness credit without contact success. A checkbox or provider acceptance never attests actual contact. Waiver/No longer needed supplies no success credit and is excluded separately from the work ratio rather than counted as failure; only explicitly recorded `contacted` and its actual occurrence supplies actual-contact evidence. Use one fixed asOf and actual immutable originating occurrence Instant as source-start. Include mature unresolved obligations at age>=48 elapsed hours as measured misses and valid successful completion at/after source-start and by source-start+48 hours (inclusive), even before maturity. Exclude younger pending, future actual origin/effective creation or completion, completion before origin, and missing/invalid origin/completion from the ratio with exact truthful labels. Waived/no_longer_needed obligations are separately excluded, not failures. Soft-deleted genuine originating meetings retain immutable historical evidence. Actual occurrence/completion and recordedAt/database createdAt are distinct: legitimate later recording never invalidates valid backdated evidence. Legacy task-createdAt-start formulas remain labelled only in their original snapshot versions. Manual or missing-origin tasks are unmeasured. Successful-contact freshness comes from actual dated contact outcomes; future/missing evidence is Unknown. Person-row edit time and email opens/clicks never prove contact. Preserve legacy snapshot versions without exposing an edit-time proxy as current contact truth.

Phase 6 retains the authoritative post-launch 48-hour guest-follow-up priority. Without an approved post-launch guest origin supplying relevant originating obligations and elapsed task-completion evidence, with actual contact success separately reported, its value is **unmeasured/Unknown**. Prelaunch Vision success cannot measure Sunday/post-launch guests. Named Launch guests and a submitted debrief alone do not create those obligations. UI, Evry, exports and assessment wording show the missing measurement boundary. No recurring-services module, automatic new follow-up generator or Phase 6 exit criterion is introduced.

Coverage uses stable responsibility mappings, not name substrings. Staffing counts filled slots; attendance counts distinct applicable active people; training counts distinct current assigned person/program obligations. Preserve the 40/35/25 engagement weights and measured thresholds; unknown components do not cause silent reweighting. Every required training program has a saved explicit participant roster. Bulk team/leader selection expands to exact reviewed people, deduplicated per program. Later membership/leadership changes do not mutate it. Explicit assignment add/remove/correction has versioned history; completion neither assigns a person nor is implied by assignment. Revocation/correction changes current completion while retaining earned history. Complete means 100% of applicable required obligations; absent applicability/empty required evidence stays Unknown, and rounding cannot clear a remaining obligation.

The leadership candidate signal requires dated evidence of 60 days of unbroken attendance **and** volunteering. Dated planter attestation can cover behavior the app cannot observe, naming the actual person, both behaviors, sustained period/as-of, question/reference version, author and correction lineage. It is a typed person attestation, not an eighth legacy toggle or private check-in. Tenure, lifetime counts or active membership is insufficient. No new quota, attendance percentage, absence waiver or expiry is added. This opens a human conversation; no automatic qualification, role assignment, promotion or phase effect. Actual candidate operational evidence is planter-only; organizations receive permitted coverage aggregates. PI/Evry/model inputs show only the presence/count/date of recorded human interviews or FourCs, never their raw scores, judgments, notes, result or qualification labels.
