# Current product scope

All retained capabilities have stable feature IDs and operation/requirement/case/package mappings in the [catalog](../catalog/features.json). Historical code helpers may map many-to-one to a shared domain operation. A source test count is not product coverage. The current acceptance catalog distinguishes defined future tests from executed evidence.

Build identity/onboarding, scoped tenant and affiliation operations, CRM People/households/interviews/4Cs/commitments/import/export/corrections/merge and safe Unmerge, ministries/roles/training, meetings/attendance/guest/response-card/follow-up, tasks/checklists/dependencies/recurrence, mail drafts/scheduling/delivery, inbox/preferences/digests, Wiki/reading/bookmarks/organization versions and notices, native/generated documents, phases and human evidence/private care, Plant and Network Intelligence/geography, Launch/practices/guests/debrief/reviewed publication, dashboard/admin/public-safe feedback, operations/recovery and native/Evry parity. Hidden/auth/admin/job/AI paths count as capability scope.

The following additions are deliberately outside the current build: finance, SMS, inbound mail, coaching and background checks, recurring post-launch services, a separate lead designation/activation/transfer/special-rejoin engine, multiple active account seats, a public roadmap requirement, broad audit browser/export, private-care assistant/model access, autonomous durable Evry actions, auto-merge/deployment and a second backup provider. Ordinary meeting/task recurrence, reminders/digests, native original-author care, manual exceptional ownership recovery outside the product and safe inverse Person Unmerge remain distinct retained needs. Native post-launch guest follow-up evidence is Unknown where no approved originating mechanism exists; prelaunch Vision measures cannot substitute.

Explicitly deferred source feature IDs IDN-018, IDN-025 and TEAM-014 are not active build obligations. EVR-267 and EVR-268 are prohibited assistant-care surfaces; the native private-care capabilities remain. Their source identifiers are traced outside active build context. Background-check fields/filters are removed from otherwise retained People/team/training reads. No other source capability disappears merely because its old mechanism is replaced or its source suite is absent.

Requirements and acceptance cases describe product end states. Default implementation choices belong to [EveryField patterns](intent://local/note/df294729-367b-493b-9faf-654cf6b25b62). Fixture labels illustrate inputs and observations; they do not require tables, API fields, counters or lock strategies.

## Accepted multiplication scope

ADR-001 stages 1 and 2, including organization-owned pipeline people, goals, conversations, apprenticeship/residency programs and the consented seatless My residency experience, ship after planter S2 and before alpha. Both sending churches and sending networks own their own pipeline. See REQ-MUL-001 through REQ-MUL-017 in [People](people.md#multiplication-pathway-contract).

Stages 3 and 4 are deferred: commissioning, planter-confirmed prefill, lineage, partnership agreements and bilateral review tasks, network totals delivery, church sending stages and revised NI inputs. Agreement tasks require an existing plant. Future network totals require per-relationship Owner/Admin opt-in, immediate withdrawal and at least two contributing organizations; no individual candidates are shared. Coaching, post-send care and cross-church candidate sharing remain outside alpha.
