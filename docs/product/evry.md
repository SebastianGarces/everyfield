# Evry operational assistance

Evry supports the whole current application through the same authorized domain operations used by native screens. It can inspect recorded work, retrieve authorized evidence, prepare changes and present exact reviews. It cannot provide pastoral or Biblical counsel, compose prayers, judge spiritual suitability, act as a responsible leader, or acquire permission from model output. A literal user-supplied task title such as “Pray for the launch” is ordinary task data.

The [operation inventory](../catalog/operations.json) keeps native and Evry behavior aligned. REQ-EVR-020 requires every operation flagged as a model tool to be exposed through its reviewed Evry adapter. The [generated model-tool list](../catalog/evry-tools.json) comes from those flags; there is no second feature row per operation. Distinct assistant requirements define conversations, context, review, execution, recovery and evaluation. Every entry names its purpose, features, producer, allowed actors and authority rule, affected records and side effects, durable-write review flag, model-tool availability, privacy exclusions and cases. Requirements and cases define behavior; builders choose signatures, internal types, transport shapes and code layout. Native and assistant adapters share the same scoped domain behavior. Task checklists use the canonical one-level child Task operations with independent assignment, status and history. Parent/child completion is independent; inert template definitions create real children after review and carry no second completion state.

## Context and authority

An account has one active seat. A conversation belongs privately to one initiating account in one exact authenticated-self account, plant or typed organization context. Switching the native page or expanding the panel retains that conversation; changing its actual tenancy context requires an explicit permitted context choice. No caller-selected ID, retained citation, summary, source text, workflow hint or model confidence can grant access. Account-private Discovery context encodes the approved native preplant floor: its own documents, currently public global Wiki and own Discovery operations. It carries no plant or organization seat, phase, People/team/health grant or native-only credential/private-care surface. This is an engineering interpretation of capability preservation, not a new user ruling. Durable own-account domain/file effects through Evry still require exact review.

Owner and Admin have equal routine authority inside their own tenancy. Only the plant Owner requests workspace deletion. A lead ministry-role Person is visible CRM/ministry information and adds no account privilege. A Member retains approved plant operational reads and current own-task outcomes/checklist authority; full interviews, four-Cs assessments and private commitment intake are restricted to authorized plant Owner/Admin. A currently seated Member linked to the recorded team leader has only the accepted own-team floor; it cannot become a general meeting/RSVP/card/delete administrator.

Organization Evry is part of the complete target. Organization Owner/Admin/Member can read only the same current bounded oversight, published-summary and Network Intelligence projections their native screens permit. Every plant contribution requires a direct active affiliation with the exact organization and currently enabled per-organization category. A network-parent seat or another affiliate supplies no inherited grant. Organization Owner/Admin reviewed profile/location/own-relationship writes use the same native functions and guard their exact organization. Neither organization scope nor plant Owner status allows raw cross-plant People, interview intake or private care. Network Intelligence purpose strips individual Person/lead-directory/interview content from older retained messages and citations as well as new DTOs. The ordinary authorized directory may show the lead ministry-role Person's display name through its separate operation; that display name is not Network Intelligence model input.

Private weekly care remains native entry, history and correction for its original author while that account holds a current same-plant Owner/Admin seat. Its content, record existence, calendar/history/nudge metadata and derived signals are absent from all assistant tools, model context, summaries, artifacts, telemetry, shared audit, organization results and Plant Intelligence. Public denial responses do not confirm guessed private IDs. No dormant coach or background-check grant, field, filter, import/export column or tool is installed.

## Exact reviewed effects

Authorized reads and nonpersistent drafts may run immediately. Every durable domain change through Evry—including a saved native draft, bookmark, notification read state, file generation/replacement, import and outbound communication—requires an exact visible trusted review and approval. The review lists targets, all arguments, exclusions, induced consequences, dates/timezone/DST choices, exact audience/outbound bytes and file/template digests. Compound meeting finalization exposes its required obligations/tasks; a meeting-invitation recipe shows meeting creation, guest binding and invitation sending together.

The assistant state protocol records explicit submitted messages, candidate review revisions and actual saved outcomes; it does not confer domain-effect authority. Model planning notes are bounded candidate context. A trusted review control binds account, exact context, proposal revision, canonical digest, grant/resource versions and expiry. Conversational “yes” alone does not create this witness. Editing targets, body, date, audience, attachment, owner or consequences supersedes the old review. Fresh authority, lifecycle, consent and revision checks run again when an unresolved effect starts.

The owning domain transaction includes authorization, mutation, history, synchronous material revision, command receipt, safe required audit and effect intent. Model/provider work stays outside that short transaction. A lost response replays the existing receipt. Provider acceptance, delivery, opens/clicks and actual human contact remain different facts. Unknown external outcomes are reconciled before any retry; they are never silently called failed or sent again under a new key.

Corrections are command-specific. Person merge retains native `people.previewUnmerge` and `people.unmergePeople`, with the corresponding exact reviewed assistant operation. A safe inverse validates the stored moved-reference/account/photo/uniqueness manifest and current revisions. Changed or irreversible downstream use produces a named conflict refusal and zero mutations. Separately reviewed exceptional correction is a fallback after refusal; it does not replace retained Unmerge. The [Person-reference registry](../architecture/person-references.json) makes every later consumer register its references and actual merge/retirement/inverse/conflict fixtures before closure.

## Finite runtime contract

The selected engineering baseline is one app-owned serial model/tool loop with typed bounded read composition. It owns one conversation, turn, saved review and execution protocol. It uses the existing shared job/receipt/effect storage. Optional SDK wrappers, external routing hints and arbitrary code interpreters are unnecessary to deliver the approved outcomes and are not mandatory dependencies. There is no general agent coding authority, workflow/policy platform or service per source helper.

The following limits are engineering constants, not a provider/model purchase or a new autonomy permission. The runtime integrator owns them and the named failure fixtures. Changing a constant requires updating the contract and rerunning affected acceptance; it never enlarges write authority.

## Evaluation and closure

The [case catalog](../catalog/index.json) registers the acceptance files by owning domain. Evry runtime examples live in [evry.json](../acceptance/evry.json); inherited and source-suite gap examples for People, meetings, documents and other domains live alongside their native cases. Each case uses named given rows, an actor, a clock, an operation and literal expected results, with named subcases for alternate paths. The case IDs are preserved across this layout. Historical expectations changed by current approved roles, organization scope or guest capture are amended explicitly; the source crosswalk preserves their originals. The pinned latest local source has 118 default bindings and 40 unbound scenarios, or 121/37 with the three optional file/CSV transports. The old 108/110/48 prose is historical evidence. Ten cases in the legacy 48 are default-bound by the latest source; target acceptance for every case remains future-not-run.

The whole evaluation corpus includes all 158 inherited cases, the 52 suite-gap cases, current UI/organization/Unmerge/runtime cases and later native corrections/privacy/integration cases. Synthetic exact tenants and fake providers prove domain effects. Each critical invitation/import/own-task/team/intelligence/notification/commitment journey runs three independent repetitions. Every repetition must pass all hard privacy, authority, exact approval, fact/effect identity, replay and delivery-truthfulness gates. A single violation blocks release regardless of average quality. Then required scores are fact exactness ≥95%, grounded citations ≥95%, useful completion ≥90%, native/assistant semantic parity 100%, p95 first useful card ≤8 seconds and bounded-read completion ≤30 seconds, with repeated overview prose absent in ≥95% of turns. A failed quality/performance gate yields an explicit native handoff and a blocked assistant release; it does not silently remove preserved capability.

The [adapter ownership manifest](../plan/adapter-ownership.json) assigns disjoint files for WP-059..086 and one kernel integrator across WP-051..055. Domain owners supply actual authorized handlers and consumer proof. The integrator alone composes the catalog/runtime/review controller and verifies forbidden grants/operation names are absent. WP-056/057 provide whole-target closure after each owning slice proves its own integration. File presence, case counts and planning checks never establish application completion.

## Public feedback boundary

Normal safe private feedback intake automatically creates and triages an issue through the named public-feedback bridge. Public publication uses a closed safe serializer with fixed product area/category and opaque occurrence data; it does not retrieve or publish private original text, attachments, logs, prompts, tokens, identities or private-care information. Authorized private triage retains the original. Unsafe/uncertain material is withheld while private intake succeeds. Subject/body enrichment requires explicit reviewed publication. The bridge owns automatic reconciliation/maintenance of its safe issue/status linkage; it grants no automatic implementation, code modification, merge or deployment authority. The externally supplied merged development workflow remains awaited and no replacement rubric is authored here.

No application/provider/account/spend/database/PR/deployment/source mutation is authorized by this design. Actual model qualification, provider privacy settings, zero-retention enrollment before paid customers, external workflow handoff and runtime/browser/provider/storage acceptance remain separate future proof gates.
