# Wiki and Church Documents

WIKI-001–011 and DOC-001–016 define current reading, personal reading state, source editing, safe exports, template generation and native owned writing. Wiki organization editorial rights and Church Documents rights are separate. Plant values/guides/internal writing uses Church Documents; there is no plant Wiki editor or third CMS. Native and Evry use the same current-entitlement resolver; each durable Evry write is exactly reviewed.

## Article resolution and editing

`wiki_article` identifies a canonical logical article, independent of title/slug. `wiki_source` is global or exact organization `(organization_id,organization_kind)`; versions bind article/source/revision and immutable safe content hash. Independent same-title/slug additions receive distinct logical IDs. Organization editing of a global article keeps its canonical ID and creates only that organization's version. The resolver returns selected published version, hidden-default, source_order_required or unavailable.

Resolve each article independently: first eligible explicit published edit/hide in the plant's stored sending-church order, then sending-network order, then global. Inherited/unchanged/missing/inactive sources fall through. Each group has one explicit sequence; its first entry is Preferred Wiki source. Setting preference moves the chosen eligible entry to head while preserving the rest, through the same revisioned `wiki.setSourceOrder` operation. Preference grants no affiliation primacy, oversight, financing or editorial authority. Invalid duplicate/foreign/wrong-kind entries and stale revision fail closed.

A winning hide suppresses the default article in list/search/navigation/related/guide/export/retrieval. An authorized source-view browser and Source dropdown still expose other independently eligible published versions, including global. The hiding source's hidden body and drafts are denied. Switching source is a read context; it does not mutate plant defaults. Unique eligible additions remain discoverable. Missing explicit order for competing sources displays source_order_required, never a name/ID/date/join-order winner. An added/rejoining affiliation recognition is a new unranked candidate; administrative explicit order resolves competition, while authorized source reading/unique additions remain. Existing inactive entries are skipped without automatically rewriting history or restoring old ranks.

Current own-organization Owner/Admin may add/edit/hide/publish its versions. No plant administrator edits global or another organization; global publication is scoped reference-operator authority. Native publishing uses expected state/version, sanitized shared editor content and meaningful-update flag; mutation/history/audit/receipt and required upstream notice agree transactionally. Global publication flows through unedited inheritance, preserving organization customizations/hides. It issues an in-app-only upstream-review notice to current organization editors for every changed published global revision, including typo-only. That notice never adopts/overwrites or becomes email due to preferences. Publisher-marked meaningful revision controls the separate reader label, not the editor trigger.

All body access is current: direct route/ID, search/snippet/count, navigation, related, source view, guide, PDF, old revision/history/citation, cache, stream, generated context and Evry retrieval. Ending one affiliation removes its bodies/history while surviving independently valid grants remain. Old bookmarks/completion/citations retain minimal identity evidence but cannot restore content. Hosted prior references redact denied excerpts and offer a current eligible source, rather than replay departed text into UI/model. Already downloaded/public external copies cannot be recalled. Cache keys bind purpose, exact scope, article/source/version and current affiliation/order/publication/access revision; private/public caches cannot share keys. Reauthorize on retrieval and response/stream continuation; invalidation alone is not a permission check.

## Reading and Discovery evidence

Completion retains actual reader, canonical article, source/version/hash, policy version, completion time and observed progress. A publisher-marked meaningful revision after that evidence shows **Updated since you read it** while keeping completed credit and allowing voluntary reread. Typo-only changes do not create the reader label; an intervening typo cannot erase an unread earlier meaningful update. Existing completion never claims the reader saw new text, resets Discovery/training or bypasses current body entitlement.

Discovery foundations is Met only when **one eligible actual reader has completed all six canonical Discovery articles at a ratio strictly greater than 0.80 for each**. An actual Owner, or an ordinary lead linked to the actual reader account with a current same-plant Admin seat, can supply the whole set under the accepted current authority. Ordinary Admin title alone cannot qualify. CRM lead title creates no authority. Owner 0/6 plus eligible actual lead/reader 6/6 qualifies through that one reader; two people each reading disjoint halves does not. Credits are never pooled. Any currently accessible published source version of the same canonical article qualifies; drafts, hidden/departed body access and unrelated same-title articles do not.

## Native owned writing

DOC-016 `owned_documents` has exactly one immutable owner: account or plant; `owned_document_revisions` inherits that owner through scoped FK. Creator attribution is not ownership. Account-private original/read/import objects stay accessible only to that active account. Plant-scoped Church Documents use current document read/write predicates under accepted plant authority; organization Wiki grants/affiliation/sharing alone never disclose them. No broad team/org grant is created by reuse of editor components.

Reuse the Wiki safe editor for native text/headings/lists/tables/links, save/render/edit, explicit `core_values` designation and revision/archive history. Ready saved nonempty designated current native text is structural core-values evidence; empty/title-only/unsaved/archived/failed parse never qualifies. No model judges theology/quality/calling/compliance or approves publication. Corrections append attributed revisions and expected-current-revision CAS. Archive removes current evidence while retaining why prior assessments used a historical revision.

Import supports UTF-8 text and modern Word DOCX parsed into editable native content, not an attachment-only link. Reject legacy binary DOC, macro-enabled documents, unsupported or malformed containers with a clear format/retry path. Engineering bounds: upload ≤10 MiB, DOCX expanded ≤40 MiB, ≤2,000 ZIP entries, ≤2 million extracted text characters and 15-second parser timeout. Check container signatures/path traversal/expansion, discard scripts/macros/external relationships/embedded executables, never fetch remote resources, and sanitize native nodes/URLs. Preserve private import provenance/source hash. Failed/empty import leaves prior ready revision/evidence intact. No OCR/conversion service or perfect Word layout promise.

The explicit native **Use for this church** command copies one selected authorized account revision into one actual authorized plant-owned document with immutable source lineage, preserving the private original. It never copies all account assets, transfers ownership silently or guesses a plant in a multi-context account. The selected copy becomes plant-owned under normal document policy; later original edits do not silently update it. Copy/history/audit/receipt share the DB transaction and any byte references follow the upload lifecycle. Plant-owned docs survive creator-account deletion; account-owned originals follow that account's private deletion lifecycle. Private care is never a document/model source.
