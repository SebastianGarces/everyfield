# EveryField Network Intelligence rubric v1

EveryField authored this first portfolio reference on 13 October 2026 under the approved separate-rubric direction. It interprets operational evidence in one exact sending-church/network portfolio. It is separate from the original SME Plant Intelligence rubric and Playbook; those originals are unchanged. This reference now has concrete rules and synthetic expected examples. Model evaluation and production activation are future gates, and no passing application evaluation is claimed.

The complete structured authority is [network-rubric-v1.json](network-rubric-v1.json). There are no weights, health scores or plant rankings. The model phrases supported deterministic rule results and next steps; it does not calculate numbers, create observations, invent testimony or make pastoral judgments.

## Input eligibility and support

A contribution needs a current exact-organization relationship or valid existing grant, health/results `shared_findings` ON, an organization-released PI result where used, and all underlying input-category grants. Health ON never overrides a restricted People/meeting/team/task source. Each pattern at each Region, state/province, Country and Whole portfolio level requires two distinct plants with relevant observed evidence for that specific rule/key. Two directory members, two sharing plants or duplicate evidence from one plant are insufficient.

An independently allowed nonsharing plant row shows name/link and **Not shared**, with no protected state/reason/value or denominator/support credit. Missing evidence for a sharing plant remains Unknown, not zero, failed or healthy. Real observed zero remains observed zero. Only known observed values enter the substantive denominator, labeled with available-evidence scope; two Unknown plants cannot manufacture a substantive pattern. Singletons can retain their authorized bounded lens row without a pattern.

Whole portfolio is the selected organization's independently deduplicated eligible cohort. Country is a separate actual-country grouping. US Region means one correctable primary official metro/micro CBSA code/type/release, starting July2023/OMB23-01; multistate Regions remain intact while state groups use actual state. Unresolved geography can still contribute to eligible known larger levels/Whole portfolio. No foreign data, census-wide benchmark, street address, coordinates, additional consent or anonymity claim enters this rubric.

## Finding rules

### NIR-01 · Recurring shared concern

Released organization-facing structured PI concern with stable concern_key and lens_key; all source-category grants passed. For a fixed concern_key, count distinct eligible plants with that actual released concern. At least two support a pattern; no diagnosis is invented from a label.

Permitted numerical claims: support_count, observed_lens_count, support_count / observed_lens_count when denominator > 0. Each uses exact persisted aggregate/window/citation.

Example template: “{support_count} plants share this recorded {concern_key} concern among {observed_lens_count} plants with available shared evidence for this lens.”

Suggested next step: Offer an organization support conversation about this recorded launch-progress concern; leave cause and pastoral judgment to humans.

### NIR-02 · Recurring shared strength

Released organization-facing structured strength with stable lens_key and strength_key; current eligible evidence. At least two distinct eligible plants have the same actual shared strength_key. Unknown/not_shared never count as a strength.

Permitted numerical claims: support_count, observed_lens_count, support_count / observed_lens_count when denominator > 0. Each uses exact persisted aggregate/window/citation.

Example template: “{support_count} plants have a shared {strength_key} strength in the available evidence. A conversation may help the organization learn what they found useful.”

Suggested next step: Offer a voluntary exchange of recorded operational practice through authorized plant links; do not infer transferability, qualifications or guaranteed outcomes.

### NIR-03 · Near-launch preparation support

Actual uncompleted launch status/date plus deterministically unresolved assignment or required-training gap, with full underlying category provenance. For each eligible plant, 0 <= plant-local days_to_launch <= 30 AND (known unfilled required responsibility count > 0 OR known outstanding applicable required training obligations > 0). At least two qualifying distinct plants support the pattern. Near date alone does not qualify; missing gap/date is Unknown.

Permitted numerical claims: support_count, observed_launch_preparation_count, support_count / observed_launch_preparation_count when denominator > 0. Each uses exact persisted aggregate/window/citation.

Example template: “{support_count} plants within 30 calendar days of an uncompleted launch have a recorded assignment or required-training gap.”

Suggested next step: Coordinate a bounded preparation support conversation with the affected authorized plants. Do not move dates, change phase, fill roles or claim launch failure.

### NIR-04 · Follow-up ownership support

Shared aggregates of actual live originating-meeting follow-up obligations/tasks: overdue_count and unowned_overdue_count, actual local due dates; all underlying categories allowed. At least two eligible distinct plants have observed overdue_count > 0. A separate unowned subclaim requires at least two distinct plants with unowned_overdue_count > 0. Completion/contact/waiver are distinct.

Permitted numerical claims: support_count, sum_overdue_obligations, unowned_support_count only when >=2, sum_unowned_overdue_obligations only when unowned_support_count >=2. Each uses exact persisted aggregate/window/citation.

Example template: “{support_count} plants have {sum_overdue_obligations} recorded overdue follow-up obligations in the available shared evidence.”

Suggested next step: Offer help reviewing existing task ownership and overdue obligations; do not blame a person or claim unsuccessful contact from task status.

### NIR-05 · Genuine-acquisition momentum support

Known valid first-adult-acquisition clock from historical genuine acquisitions, independently of current headcount; uncertainty/provenance carried. Generate stalled theme only with at least two eligible distinct plants whose confirmed clock >=28 days. Generate slowed theme only with at least two whose confirmed clock is >=21 and <28 days. Do not double count stalled plants in slowed theme. Unresolved possible newer acquisition prevents old-date negative claim.

Permitted numerical claims: support_count, observed_growth_clock_count, support_count / observed_growth_clock_count when denominator > 0. Each uses exact persisted aggregate/window/citation.

Example template: “{support_count} plants show {momentum_label} in their available first-adult-acquisition evidence as of {as_of}.”

Suggested next step: Offer an operational growth conversation using the recorded timing; do not call a church unhealthy, forecast acquisition, infer conversions or erase genuine acquisitions after withdrawal.

### NIR-06 · Required-training coordination

Saved current required person/program assignments and earned/revoked/corrected completion aggregates, required_applicability_known and nonempty obligation_count. At least two eligible distinct plants with complete applicability evidence and outstanding_required_count > 0 support the pattern. Current roster is explicit, not recalculated from membership. Missing/empty applicability is Unknown, never 100%.

Permitted numerical claims: support_count, sum_outstanding_required_obligations, sum_applicable_required_obligations, sum_current_complete_required_obligations / sum_applicable_required_obligations only for identical reported observed cohort with denominator > 0. Each uses exact persisted aggregate/window/citation.

Example template: “{support_count} plants have {sum_outstanding_required_obligations} outstanding saved required-training obligations in the available shared evidence.”

Suggested next step: Offer coordination around existing assigned programs; no automatic assignment, completion, role qualification or phase effect.

## Evidence and output

All numbers, dates and proportions come from persisted deterministic aggregate facts. A citation binds exact org/group/run, fact path/value, numerator/denominator/formula, source/reference generation, time window and as-of. If a required path/value/support/window cannot be verified, reject the draft before publication. Percent display may round to one decimal but never creates a readiness pass or a missing denominator. No growth cap, forecast or conversion claim is part of v1.

The judge receives aggregate facts, availability, rule results and this whole rubric. Server-only provenance proves contributor support. The server attaches only currently allowed plant name, shared lens state, short reason from an actually shared structured finding and canonical authorized link. An aggregate number already inside a shared reason may remain; a new numeric pattern claim uses an organization/group citation. No per-plant metric table, raw assessments, individual people/interviews/4Cs, source composition, private notes or private-care content/existence/history/calendar/nudge metadata reaches prompts/results/evaluation. Names and links are allowed bounded product fields; they are not raw roster or access grants.

Unknown output says what evidence is available and what is missing. “No supported pattern” never means the portfolio is healthy. A near launch date alone does not warn; a task tick does not prove contact; a real withdrawal never erases a genuine acquisition; incomplete/empty training applicability is not complete. Suggested actions remain operational conversations or existing reviewed workflows, with no automatic task/message/phase/role change or cause assigned to a person. All eligible finding records are retained in stable rule/group/key order, and a compact UI can paginate. Plant rows are never ranked.

## Activation, feedback and verification

[Network examples](network-examples.json) pin synthetic inputs, exact rule/support/group outputs, uncertainty and forbidden-field expectations. Static reference validation can check hashes, rule arithmetic and expected contributor sets; it is separate from future model semantic evaluation. WP041 owns immutable authored content, WP042 evaluates it, WP043 shares the bounded assessment demand/lease/publication machinery, WP047 supplies exact-org projections and WP044/073/074 render/read/review writes. Model/provider/scheduler proof executes later.

A candidate is active only after complete reference-generation validation and explicit publisher activation; failed candidates leave the prior active pointer intact. Rollback is a new activation pointing to prior validated immutable bytes, never rewritten history. Feedback belongs to the actual authorized reader and exact org/run/finding/rubric version; it does not automatically change criteria, scores, models or activation. Every durable Evry action uses exact human review. Current grants are rechecked at publication and every current/history/export/cache/tool/link read. Revocation withholds obsolete contributed patterns immediately; a stale label alone grants nothing.
