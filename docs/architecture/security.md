# Authorization, privacy and security

Authorization is enforced at the server data boundary and again inside durable mutations and deferred effects. A valid credential alone grants no tenant access. Exact actor, current authority for the selected account or tenant context, typed subject owner, purpose, current relationship/category grants and lifecycle govern every read, list/count/search/export/RSC/stream/file/history and Evry path. Authenticated-self account/Discovery access needs no tenancy seat and creates no tenant grant; tenant authority still permits at most one active seat. Member duties, ordinary ministry leadership and discovery relationships never lend Owner/Admin authority; organization seats never become plant administrators. Owner/Admin share routine plant administration and Owner alone requests plant deletion.

## Data isolation and projections

Every child and reference belongs to the correct plant, account or organization. Missing or nullable owners cannot bypass isolation; organization-kind endpoints and scoped uniqueness remain enforced. Ownership is immutable. A reviewed selected copy creates a new owned document rather than transferring the original. Every Person-bearing consumer has explicit merge, retirement and inverse behavior with fixtures before closure.

A selected document copy preserves source-version provenance without granting source-body access or an ongoing dependency. Fresh source-account access and target-plant Owner/Admin authority create new destination-owned revisions and bytes. Source purge removes the live provenance link while preserving the allowed content hash. Current content and object bindings remain same-owner. Private documents, files, assistant content and recipient data purge by their actual private owner; independently owned plant/org content may survive with minimum attribution.

Profile writes accept only known fields and bounded nested values. Plant locality and description may clear independently without inferred geography/timezone; street remains outside organization directory/oversight. Exact own-org Owner/Admin alone edit profile/name/contact with conflict detection and audit. Immutable organization kind/identity, system fields and unknown nested keys are rejected. Editing grants no wider CRM or intelligence access.

Affiliation A and B are independent current exact pairs/recognition episodes. A's sharing ON cannot satisfy B. A sending church's network link confers no plant/network grant. Ended/history/pending bridges confer no current reach. Health/results `affiliation.shared_findings` is the same permission for released health and Network Intelligence; other category restrictions still apply. Missing required policy fails closed. NI has no individual people/private narratives/check-ins and no raw contributor-manifest DTO; bounded named lens rows carry only actually shared state/reason and page link. Every pattern at each geographic level needs two distinct currently eligible contributors.

NI copies remain governed by their source plants. A plant deletion request or sharing/affiliation revocation immediately denies affected saved inputs, current/history findings and derivatives, advances the exact organization revision and fences pending jobs/publication. At eligible plant live purge, enumerate affected runs from protected contributor rows, withhold affected derived current results, then purge each whole mixed run/result, all its contributors, linked feedback and registered copied bodies. This includes `assessment_fact`, `finding`, `assessment_contributor`, protected run/input manifests and exported/artifact copies. Other plants and the organization survive and can recompute; there is no preserved body snapshot in organization history. Only separate minimum organization receipt metadata may remain. Every recovery copy containing the removed source obeys the original plant live-purge plus seven-day expiry, without reset by organization ownership, recopy or restore; apply the current witness before any restored access. Cancellation never revives a stale result or independently revoked grant.

Purpose-specific projections define permitted output. Raw account/Person/interview/assessment rows never reach client props, JSON/errors, HTML metadata, stream chunks or model context by accident. Counts and errors cannot reconstruct hidden identities or private-care existence. Public Wiki crawler previews expose only approved global content regardless of user agent. Organization Wiki body entitlement is rechecked on current/history/citation/print/download/chat/source/preview access; completed reading or old citation never grants departed-body access.

Private response caching cannot expose a prior caller's data or bypass current entitlement, lifecycle or purpose rules. Personalized responses and downloads cannot enter public caches. Any future shared private cache requires proof of actor/scope/projection separation and current-entitlement invalidation. Global reference caching remains separate from effective organization corpus access. Default cache mechanisms follow [EveryField patterns](intent://local/note/df294729-367b-493b-9faf-654cf6b25b62).

## Private native care

Only the original authenticated account author, currently eligible Owner/Admin in that exact plant, can create/read/correct/history their own check-ins. Another Owner/Admin, a changed lead title or platform grant cannot open them. Author+plant+stable logical Monday week is unique; author/start/zone/ordinal are immutable. Confirmed personal timezone is separate from the plant calendar; missing preference returns an explicit timezone-required result.

The account maintains one private logical period. Zone change previews a pending transition to the first strictly future Monday in the requested zone, retains the current identity until that boundary, then opens exactly the next ordinal. Serialize rollover before zone edit/answer save; repeated pending edits replace one proposal. Missing answer weeks remain logical gaps; date-line edits cannot manufacture adjacent-week/streak credit. Three adjacent answered weeks of Strained OR Struggling in the same author/dimension trigger only the native neutral historical support question; Steady or a missing week breaks the run. There is no dispatch/task/phase action.

Answers, notes, history and existence/period/calendar/nudge metadata are excluded from broad audit, logs/traces, email, exports, organization reports, models/Evry/tool/context/output/evaluations and PI assessment input changess. Private correction history remains bound to the original author and cannot enter general correction or audit history. Account/plant purge removes private rows/bytes under the approved lifecycle.

## Request and provider boundaries

Database/auth/storage/mail/model adapters are server-only. Client components import safe types/DTOs, never adapters or secrets. Reachable Server Actions and cookie-authenticated routes authorize themselves; layouts/proxies/action IDs only aid navigation. Same-origin browser mutations retain framework/Better Auth CSRF protections plus exact public scheme/host/port policy and trusted ingress headers. Missing/foreign Origin is rejected for browser mutations. Signed provider callbacks, sealed unsubscribe/RSVP capabilities and machine executor triggers have narrow named independently authenticated exceptions. GET/render operations are side-effect free.

Server parsers allowlist IDs, fields, filters/order and bounded sizes; trim required display names and distinguish omitted from explicit null. Public auth/upload/model/mail boundaries enforce atomic or explicitly bounded quotas with trusted client-IP extraction. Errors return stable safe codes, never SQL/stack/provider payloads. Password/recovery/mailbox proof does not enumerate another account or permit mass assignment.

Uploads require an authorized immutable-owner intent and fresh finalize. Keys are opaque server-generated IDs; file names, extensions and caller MIME are not proof. Validate actual format/signature/bytes/checksum, compressed-expansion/renderer/parser limits and exact owner/purpose before attachment. Downloads reauthorize current scope and lifecycle. Use an access-checked no-store proxy where prompt revocation matters; signed URLs have finite expiry and cannot promise immediate revocation after issuance. Office/CSV parsing neutralizes risky content and formula injection; HTML/MDX/editor previews use reviewed sanitization/CSP and no arbitrary trusted script execution.

Resend callbacks authenticate raw bytes and timestamp with the supported verifier and match the actual expected provider message. Duplicate or out-of-order callbacks cannot alter foreign work, resend mail or regress delivery. Unknown acceptance requires provider evidence before another attempt. Disconnect, cancellation or process loss cannot erase or duplicate already committed work. Provider latency cannot hold up unrelated authorized changes.

## Environments, operators and AI processing

Local/test/preview/staging/production have separate database, auth, object namespaces, sender/callback credentials, model budgets and triggers. No preview inherits production private rows; fixture bootstrap/reset is explicit and protected, never startup wipe/migration. Ordinary runtime access has minimum privileges and cannot perform migrations or privileged cleanup/backup. Those capabilities remain separately restricted. Isolation must survive pooled requests and missing context, including global and relationship boundaries; the patterns choose the enforcement mechanism.

Logs/traces keep only safe correlation/outcome fields. Optional tracing does not expand model-input authority or private-data retention. Every model destination/tool/trace receives minimum currently authorized data; feedback, files and reference text are untrusted instructions and cannot forge actor, approval or SQL authority. Application history remains app-owned; optional Responses storage is disabled where the selected API contract supports it. disabled provider response storage is not evidence of zero data retention.

Development program enrollment/complimentary credits are user-stated until an authorized operator verifies actual organization/project settings and applicable contract. Shared-program development uses synthetic or confirmed permitted non-sensitive/non-confidential/non-proprietary inputs/outputs; free pilots are subject to the same restrictions. Before paid-customer AI processing, disable optional program sharing and verify the actual project/organization ZDR and approved endpoint/model/region/cache/SDK path. If absent/unverified, affected customer-data inference remains blocked while synthetic evaluation may continue. State actual verified training/sharing separately from abuse retention and optional provider state in the privacy policy once, rather than repeating disclosure in ordinary product workflows. This consolidation performs no provider verification or customer-data processing.

During the build, recovery relies on native seven-day Neon and Tigris history. Before customer data, encrypted daily backup/restore/expiry uses the same artifact's restricted operator context. Separate private Tigris backup bucket and scoped keys are unavailable to ordinary Next app/build/preview; key recovery and controlled execution/schedule are required future setup proof. Same-provider correlated failure risk is accepted. Ordinary measured coherent DB+file targets are RPO one hour/RTO four hours; a fresh surviving accessible daily copy offers 24 hour fallback with no disaster RTO/SLA. Thirty-day cancellation then live purge plus seven-day copy eligibility apply to account/plant deletion; no new public organization-deletion or audit-retention exception is introduced.
